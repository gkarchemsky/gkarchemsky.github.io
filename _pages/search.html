Search

{%- comment -%} Named, because the header carries a `role="search"` landmark too and this page therefore has two of them. Duplicate landmarks of one role have to be distinguishable, or a screen-reader user picking "search" from the landmark list cannot tell which one they are about to land in. `autofocus` stays. It is the one page on the site whose entire purpose is this input — the heading says "Search" and there is nothing else here — and every search engine behaves the same way. The usual objection, that it front-runs the skip link, assumes a page with content to skip to. {%- endcomment -%}
