{%- comment -%} CTF write-ups have their own page and their own archive, so this list is everything else. Built with a loop rather than `where_exp`, because Liquid cannot negate `contains` — `"p.categories contains 'ctf' == false"` parses and then quietly matches nothing. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign blog_posts = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for p in site.posts -%} {%- unless p.categories contains 'ctf' -%} {%- assign blog_posts = blog_posts | push: p -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- endfor -%}
